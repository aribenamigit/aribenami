## Context

Every referral tool we looked at in 2021 lived in a pop-up and asked the customer to create an account, download an app, or wait for a friend to buy. Prosper's bet was that a referral should take twenty seconds and happen where the customer already is: on the product page, in the cart, after checkout. The widget is the whole product from the shopper's side.

## Role

I designed every version of it, wrote the specs the developers built from, and did the frontend on the client side: placing it on each store, matching the store's brand, writing the integration docs. Dylan sold it; Matt and Huseyin built the backend.

## What I built

**MVP, October 2022.** Verify with a phone number or email, choose text, email or link, get a reward code immediately. I handed it to the developers with an 18-page spec: responsive widths, the two-factor flow, invalid-input states, the exact copy, and the Figma variables for theming. It shipped and clients used it, and it had problems I could watch happening: two-factor added friction, the steps looked alike, nobody knew how far along they were, and the buttons were too small on phones.

**Second widget, 2023.** A redesign from those observations. Instead of a panel, it opens as a slider ("WAIT... get $10 off your order!") that sits in the page. A progress bar runs across every step. Each step has a title. Sharing grew from text, email and link to WhatsApp, Slack, Instagram, Facebook and Reddit. Reward generation got a loading state so the customer knows something is happening. The buttons got big. Two sizes shipped: a compact one for product pages and an extended one with more context up front and a shorter path to the referral, and the store picks.

**Placements.** The widget had to look native on every store, so for each client I built the placement: the product page button on B Draddy, Fairway & Greene and Takeya; the cart drawer on Unit 1; a dedicated referral page; the Shopify order-status page. For Home Means Nevada, a Reno retailer with physical stores, that meant printed QR signs at the checkout counter and a referral text message that landed in the friend's phone.

## Outcome

Deployed on B Draddy, Fairway & Greene, Takeya, Thermoflask, Home Means Nevada, Ponto, Carico, Unit 1, Ade Dehye and Handful. B Draddy's 30-day pilot: 63 referrals, 38 conversions, about $6,300 in revenue, $1.90 per acquired customer. Across the beta, roughly a quarter of discounts given out became a sale.

## Lesson

The first version was designed from what I thought a referral should be. The second was designed from watching people get stuck in the first. The second one was better, and it took a year of shipping to earn it.
